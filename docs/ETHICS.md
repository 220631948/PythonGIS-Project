# Ethical Guidelines - Cape Town GeoHub

## Data Privacy
The Cape Town GeoHub is committed to protecting the privacy of Cape Town's residents. 

### Aggregation Policy
- **Crime Data**: To prevent the stigmatization of specific households or small businesses, crime data is only displayed as an aggregate at the **precinct or suburb level**. No individual incident locations are exposed.
- **Informal Settlements**: Data regarding informal settlements is focused on service delivery gaps rather than individuals.

## Appropriate Use
This tool is intended for:
- Urban planning research.
- Academic study.
- Community awareness of service delivery.
- Environmental risk awareness.

It should **NOT** be used for:
- Predictive policing.
- Real estate speculation based on sensitive social indicators.
- Targeting individuals or specific addresses.

## Data Sources & Accuracy
- All data is sourced from official portals: **City of Cape Town Open Data** and **Western Cape Government Open Data**.
- While we strive for accuracy, spatial analysis results are approximations based on available datasets and should be verified for critical decision-making.
- Analysis results (like accessibility scores) are calculated using straight-line distances and may not account for real-world barriers like fences or restricted access.

## Accountability
For any ethical concerns or data quality reports, please contact the project maintainers via the GitHub repository.
