# User Guide - Cape Town GeoHub

Welcome to the Cape Town GeoHub. This guide will help you navigate the platform and use its various spatial analysis tools.

## Getting Started
1. **Map View**: The central area displays the map. You can pan and zoom using your mouse or the controls at the bottom right.
2. **Sidebar**: Use the sidebar to toggle data layers on and off.
3. **URL Sharing**: The map's center, zoom level, and active layers are always synced to the URL. Copy the URL to share your current view with others.

## Analysis Modules

### 💧 Service Access
- Enable the **Service Access** layer to see informal settlements color-coded by their access to water and electricity.
- **Green**: High access, **Orange**: Medium access, **Red**: Low access.
- Click a settlement to see detailed distance statistics.

### 🚌 Transport Accessibility
- **Click anywhere on the map** to analyze public transport accessibility for that location.
- Concentric rings (800m, 1600m, 2400m) will appear, showing walking distances.
- The **Transport Panel** in the sidebar will show the count of MyCiTi stops within each ring.

### 🚨 Safety Statistics
- Enable the **Safety** layer to see crime statistics aggregated by precinct.
- Darker shades indicate higher incident counts.
- *Note: Data is aggregated for privacy compliance.*

### 🌊 Environmental Risk
- Enable the **Flood Risk** layer to see suburbs intersected with flood zones.
- The color intensity represents the percentage of the suburb's area at risk.

## PWA & Offline Features
- **Install**: You can install GeoHub as an app on your mobile device or desktop for faster access.
- **Offline Mode**: GeoHub caches map tiles and datasets as you view them.
- **Data Manager**: Open the Offline Data Manager (Wifi icon in header) to download specific datasets for full offline use.

## Accessibility
- Use `Tab` to navigate through interactive elements.
- `Ctrl + S`: Toggle Sidebar.
- `Ctrl + D`: Open Offline Data Manager.
- `Escape`: Close any open dialogs.
- High-contrast colors are used for data visualization to ensure readability.
