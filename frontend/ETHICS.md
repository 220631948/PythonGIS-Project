# Data Ethics & Limitations 🛡️

_Last updated: 2026-02-19_

## 1. Principles of Use
The Cape Town GeoHub is a technical tool designed to support planning, safety, and efficient service delivery. We adhere to the following ethical principles:

*   **Privacy First:** All data involving individuals or sensitive locations is rigorously aggregated.
*   **Transparency:** We openly acknowledge the sources and limitations of our data.
*   **Do No Harm:** This tool must not be used to discriminate, redline, or unjustly target specific communities.

## 2. Safety & Crime Data (Critical)
**WARNING:** The "Safety" module utilizes aggregated crime statistics.

*   **Aggregation:** Data is displayed at the **Precinct Level** only. No exact incident locations are stored or visualised.
*   **Context:** Crime statistics are historical counts and do NOT predict future events. High crime counts in an area may reflect higher reporting rates or population density, not necessarily higher risk for a specific individual.
*   **Use Case:** This data is intended for resource allocation (e.g., "Where do we need more lighting?"), NOT for property value assessment or vigilantism.

## 3. Data Sources & Authority
*   **Primary Authority:** Western Cape Government & City of Cape Town Open Data Portal.
*   **Accuracy:** While we strive for accuracy, GIS data can be outdated. Always verify critical infrastructure details with the relevant municipal department.

## 4. Limitation of Liability
The creators of Cape Town GeoHub accept no liability for actions taken based on this map. It is a decision-support tool, not a substitute for professional surveying or legal advice.
